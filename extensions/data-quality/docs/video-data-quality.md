# Reviewing video data quality

Open **Data Quality** from Cove's navigation and choose a saved review. Use **Edit review** to configure reusable scene criteria and tag actions: it opens a drawer beside the queue, and the header's filters and scope preview the draft's criteria until you save or cancel. New reviews start at the end of the sorted queue; existing reviews keep their chosen direction.

The filter toolbar shows the effective scene query. Its **Filters** dialog includes Cove's **Custom Fields** section for video custom fields, so a queue can require or exclude videos by a custom field such as the confirmed-absent tags that **Mark absent** records; while the review is being edited, the same dialog sets the criteria the review saves. Applying the dialog normalizes custom field criteria to their field definitions, so a review saved with incomplete criteria shows the save and reset controls after its first apply. Occurrence reviews separately offer, under **Scope** in the header, **All performers**, **Specific performers**, or **Matching criteria** (with **Edit criteria**); Specific performers with none picked, or empty criteria, reviews everyone. They also offer conditions on tags attached to that performer's appearance. A scene filter describes which videos match; performer scope describes which partners within them are reviewed. Conditions on occurrence tags apply to the same performer link, independently of video tags and performer profile tags.

Copy the URL to preserve the current search, criteria, performer scope, sort, page size, page, and direction. Reloading that URL restores its query even if the saved review later changes. Changing queue criteria shows **Queue differs from the saved review** under the header: **Save to review** stores the effective queue criteria directly, and **Reset** restores the saved criteria. The card grid and tag reviews show the same. **Edit review** changes the rest of the review, such as its name, actions and direction, and saves the queue's criteria with them.

Inspect the player and current tags, then choose an action on the pad under the player. The current tags look and sort as on Cove's video page: its tag badges, coloured by the tag or its group, grouped tags first. Point at an action, or focus it, to see what it would change: the line above the pad says it in words, and the current tags show additions with "+" and a dashed outline, removals with "−" and struck through, and new absences marked "absent". Actions apply and advance by default. Hold Shift while clicking or pressing the action's key to stay, or use the pin button that appears on the action's tile. Multiple matching scene partners are processed before switching videos, and selecting another partner preserves playback.

Each action has a letter key, in the order of the review's actions: **q w e r t y u i o p å**, **a s d f g h j k l ö ä**, then **z x c v b**. Press the key to apply the action and move on, or, in the single-item review, **Shift** and the key to apply it and stay. Press **-** for **Find action** to reach any action by name, including those after the 27th: type part of the name, choose with the arrow keys, press **Enter** to apply and move on or, in the single-item review, **Shift+Enter** to apply and stay, and **Esc** to close. In the multiple-video grid, **Ctrl+A** (**⌘A** on a Mac) selects every card on the page or clears the selection. The pad places each action where its key sits on the keyboard, leaves out rows without actions, and dims free keys; unassigned **f**, **g** and **k** name what Cove does with them instead under the Cove Native preset. The keys are fixed and work under every Cove keyboard preset; they are not listed in Cove's **?** overview or under **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts** and cannot be rebound. Holding a key down applies its action once. While the review has an action on **f**, **g** or **k**, that key applies it on this page instead of Cove's Filters or fullscreen, go-to chords, or play/pause.

To offer every tag of a category as an action, open **Edit review → Actions** and choose **Add from parent tags…**. Pick one or more parent tags and tick the child tags to turn into actions. They are listed most used first, and children that an action already adds start unticked. When a video, or in occurrence reviews a performer, can have only one of the children, tick **Only one per video** or **Only one per performer**: each action then also removes every other tag in that parent's tree, including tags created under it later. The new actions are added after the existing ones; reorder, rename, or delete them like any other action, and use the command again to add children created since.

Use **Edit tags** to add or remove any tags without changing configured actions. **Save** stays on the item, **Save & next** advances, and **Cancel** discards the draft. Inputs remain available if saving fails. If some changes succeeded before an error, inspect the refreshed current tags and retry to finish the intended edit.

**Skip** changes only the cursor. It does not save tags, reviewed status, or an inconclusive answer. Skipped items and items that still match remain available in their sorted pages. The header shows the matching scene range and total, without completion percentages; its **‹ page / pages ›** pager changes pages, and clicking the page number lets you type one.

## Review many videos at once

**Grid** in the header shows the queue as cards; **Single** returns to one video at a time. The grid keeps the same header, fits the window, and puts the actions in a bar floating over the bottom of the cards. Select cards with their checkmark or **Space**, or all cards on the page with **Select all** (**Ctrl+A**); **Clear** (**Esc**) empties the selection. With cards selected, an action applies to all of them; with none, to the focused card, as the bar says. Move with the arrow keys and press **Enter** to preview a video: **N** and **M** step to the previous and next card, the player takes **Space**, the arrows and **,**/**.**, and **Enter** or **Esc** closes it. **Cards** | **Wall** switches to autoplaying previews, and the size control next to the page size resizes the cards. When the review has **Queue tag bins**, the chip row lists the bins found on the page, **On this page**, with their counts: press one to review only videos with that tag, and again to return.

## Work performer by performer

Occurrence reviews can rank performers by the work left for them. Under **Scope**, set **Performers to review** to the performers that matter, for example **Matching criteria** with a gender, and set the occurrence condition to the categories to fill: **Missing at least one selected tag**, with one condition tag per category, finds appearances that still miss any of them. Switch the queue sidebar to **Performers** to list those performers with the most matching videos first, and choose one to review only that performer. **Existing answers** shows what the performer already has in each category. Check the earliest and latest videos, then use **Batch…** with that performer's answers, ticking one answer per category. The batch fills only categories that are still empty, so an occurrence that already has a different answer keeps it. **New batch** discards the results and starts over; closing the dialog discards them too and recounts that performer, so the next one is on top when you switch back to **Performers**. **Show all performers** returns to the whole scope.

Flag performers whose attributes change during their career with **Performer flags** in **Edit review → Review**. A flagged performer is marked in the ranking and, while chosen, on its chip, in the item column and in **Apply to all matching occurrences**, whose preview links their earliest and latest videos: review their videos by date, or narrow a batch with a date filter, instead of applying one answer to all of them.

## Audio reviews

Choose **Audios** or **Audio performer occurrence tags** as a review's entity type to review audios. Everything above applies, with the audio list's own filter criteria, sort options, and custom fields, and with tags written to the audio or to one performer's appearance in it.

Audios have no card grid, so both audio review kinds always review one audio at a time. The **Grid** layout and the grid's settings under **Edit review → Appearance** (**Cards** | **Wall**, **Select every card when a page opens**, **Card details**, **Tags on cards** and **Queue tag bins**) are offered only for video reviews.

Because an audio shows nothing while it plays, its description sits directly under the player rather than behind a tab, in both audio review kinds. The **Description** control collapses and expands it; that choice is remembered in the browser and is not saved with the review. An audio with no description says so instead of hiding the panel.

Tag-group reviews use the same grid and action bar, with **Cards** | **List**, and keep their selection and group assignment controls. Review configuration is account-scoped where permitted, with browser recovery support. Legacy video/occurrence progress remains stored but is ignored by the filter-driven workspace.
